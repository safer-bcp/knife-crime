Knife crime: help and support

Whether you are a young person, a parent or carer, or someone affected by knife crime, you do not have to deal with it alone. Call any of the numbers below.

Someone in danger or injured?Call the police or an ambulance999
55Can’t speak safely? Call 999, listen, and press 55 when prompted.
101Not an emergency? Call 101 for Dorset Police.
0800Know something? Tell Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111. You stay anonymous.

Fearless (Crimestoppers)

24/7

Tell someone about knife crime, or a worry about a friend, without giving your name. You cannot be traced.

Open 24 hours, every day

Call0800 555 111Give information onlinefearless.org
More about Fearless (Crimestoppers)

Fearless is the youth service of the independent charity Crimestoppers. Young people can pass on information about crime, raise a concern about a friend, or get advice if they are involved in something they want to get out of.

It is 100% anonymous: no personal details are taken, and calls and online forms cannot be traced. Adults can use the same number to report to Crimestoppers.

Fearless does not replace the emergency services. If someone is in danger now, call 999.

BCP Council Children’s Services

Worried a child or young person is at risk, carrying a knife or being exploited? Talk to the First Response Hub.

First Response Hub: Mon–Thu 8.30am–5.15pm, Fri 8.30am–4.45pm · Out of hours: Mon–Fri 5pm–9am, weekends and bank holidays

First Response Hub · Call01202 123 334Out of hours · Emergencies only01202 738 256
More about BCP Council Children’s Services

The Children’s First Response Hub gives advice, information and support to the public and professionals about vulnerable or at-risk children in Bournemouth, Christchurch and Poole. You can also email childrensfirstresponse@bcpcouncil.gov.uk.

Only call the out-of-hours team if a child’s safety or welfare is at serious risk of significant harm and it cannot wait until 9am the next working day.

Connection: NHS mental health helpline

24/7

Struggling to cope, or worried about someone’s mental health? Talk to Dorset HealthCare’s helpline, any time, any age.

Open 24 hours, every day

Call0800 652 0190Or call NHS 111 · Mental health option111
More about Connection: NHS mental health helpline

Connection gives support, advice and signposting to people in Dorset, including Bournemouth, Christchurch and Poole, who are struggling with their mental health. You can also call for advice about a friend or family member.

Adults can also visit The Retreat in Bournemouth, a drop-in for face-to-face support out of hours. No appointment is needed; check the website for opening times.

Changes Are Made (C.A.M)

Local knife awareness charity set up in memory of Cameron Hamilton. Talks for schools, colleges and families.

Based in Bournemouth · Contact by email

changesaremade@outlook.comSend an emailWebsitechangesaremade.com
More about Changes Are Made (C.A.M)

Changes Are Made was founded by Tracy Jose after her grandson Cameron was fatally stabbed in Bournemouth in 2023. The charity works to help, educate and deter young people from carrying knives, and to open up conversations between young people, parents, carers and teachers.
